## Why it stopped

`docs/01-spec/vault-format.md` reserves wrapped-DEK method 3 and requires
SLIP-39 group configuration/share metadata without share material, but it
does not specify the method-data byte encoding, version/bounds, relationship
to method 1 or 2, exact metadata binding, share-passphrase policy, or
older-reader behavior. `docs/03-guides/inheritance-planning.md` describes
the intended second unlock route but does not settle those wire details.

That is a vault-format and secret-unlock boundary. Under `AGENTS.md` §4 and
`docs/05-development/batch-run.md` §5, I stopped rather than inventing a
format. The proposed options and recommended direction are recorded in
[ADR-0040](../adr/0040-vault-recovery-share-record.md). The P2.5 branch has
no product implementation and no generated secret material.

## Scrutinise hardest

P2.4’s finite-field and bit/hex conversion compatibility is the highest-risk
completed implementation. For the blocked leg, the hardest unresolved issue
is method-3 record binding: a valid SLIP-39 set must be tied to the intended
vault without putting share material in the vault or silently accepting an
unrelated set.
