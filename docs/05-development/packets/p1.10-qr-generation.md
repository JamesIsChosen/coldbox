## Scope

This branch adds the QR Studio flow for public addresses and secret SeedQR artifacts. Standard SeedQR uses zero-padded four-digit BIP-39 word indices. Compact SeedQR uses the original entropy bytes. Secret QR output remains in the cold realm; public address QR output remains in the warm realm. The QR encoder is read from the existing pinned vendor tarball during the deterministic build.

Changed product areas include `src/cold/qr.js`, the cold QR Studio UI and styles, the warm public-address QR UI, the build injection points, Seed Forge word-index conversion, the QR guide, glossary wording, changelog, roadmap, and QR unit tests.

## Acceptance evidence

The roadmap acceptance text is the P1.10 line in `docs/05-development/ROADMAP.md`; the detailed format and security requirements are in SPEC §11.1. The implementation and tests cover:

- standard QR numeric encoding for valid 12/15/18/21/24-word phrases;
- compact QR binary encoding for valid 16/20/24/28/32-byte entropy;
- rejection of invalid lengths and out-of-range word indices;
- cold-realm-only secret QR generation, download, print, and session clearing;
- warm public Bitcoin and EVM address QR generation with optional URI payloads;
- user-facing warnings and the secret-confirmation gate;
- single-file build assembly and placeholder resolution.

## Security and assumptions

- The QR encoder remains the existing pinned vendored dependency; no runtime fetch was added.
- Compact SeedQR follows SPEC §11.1 and the corrected glossary wording: the payload is original entropy bytes, not raw word indices.
- No scanner or camera input is introduced in P1.10.
- Secret QR generation is gated by explicit confirmation and is cleared with the vault session.

The least-confidence areas are the browser-only print/download behavior and the interaction between the vendored QR source and the repository-wide machine-path test. The latter is a blocking, reproducible test failure, not an accepted gap.
