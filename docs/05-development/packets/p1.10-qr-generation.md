## Scope

This branch contains the P1.10 QR Studio implementation and the remediation of the independent review findings. Public Bitcoin QR payloads stay in the warm shell. Secret Standard SeedQR and Compact SeedQR generation, export, print request, and cleanup stay inside the opaque cold frame.

The remediation makes Ethereum EIP-681 amounts integer wei, rejects the unsupported Ethereum label field, makes Compact SeedQR default to SeedSigner-compatible low correction, rejects non-English Standard SeedQR, grants only `allow-modals` for cold printing, preserves wallet-sized print dimensions, uses short-lived Blob URLs for cold exports, and resets QR controls during lock teardown. The browser harness now drives the actual warm and cold QR Studio workflows in Chromium and Firefox.
