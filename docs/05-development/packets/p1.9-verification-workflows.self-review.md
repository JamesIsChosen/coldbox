## Remediation gate

The remediation addresses every agent-workable finding:

- F9: the replacement packet has no blank line at EOF and the stacked diff
  passes `git diff --check`.
- F10: the browser harness explicitly opens each collapsed `<details>` before
  interaction and checks attached/reset state after lock.
- F11: Verify Bench no longer accepts a mnemonic or passphrase. Seed Forge is
  authoritative, and focused tests prove leading, trailing, and internal
  passphrase spaces remain distinct identities.
- F12: strict printable-ASCII and checksum-aware public parsing rejects
  fullwidth text, whitespace, malformed xpubs, mixed-case Bech32, and bad
  checksums.
- F15: **Use current Seed Forge wallet** derives the active cold-local wallet
  to a public-only identity, shows all requested public ranges and xpub
  families, and invalidates on Seed Forge replacement, network change, lock,
  panic, and teardown.
- F16: the redundant standalone BIP-39 passphrase panel and duplicate secret
  inputs are gone; browser and source tests prove the absence.

handoff/invalidation paths and the assembled cold script's secret boundary.

mobile, Safari, Tor, and the physical hardware gate remain untested. A manual
public-value match is not device authenticity proof. The independent review
must re-run the complete gates from a fresh checkout and decide whether the
